Ahmad Rahdari

PhD researcher in security and privacy

Department of Computer Engineering and Mathematics
Universitat Rovira i Virgili, Tarragona, Spain

I am a doctoral researcher and FPI grant holder at Universitat Rovira i Virgili (URV), Spain, supervised by Dr. Rolando Trujillo Rasúa. My doctoral work is on post-quantum cryptographic protocols and data provenance, in particular post-quantum TLS oracles.

Prior to joining URV, I worked as a remote research assistant at the University of Greenwich, UK, on security for AI and AI for security, and as a cybersecurity researcher at the Iran Telecommunication Research Center (ITRC) on A National Framework for Cybersecurity Specialist Development, Training, and Support. I received my B.Sc. in 2018 and my M.Sc. in 2022, both in Electrical Engineering (Communication Systems), from K. N. Toosi University of Technology, Tehran, Iran. Since 2024, I have been serving as a reviewer for prominent journals such as IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, Computer Networks and Vehicular Communications.

Index Terms—Post-quantum cryptography, TLS oracles, data provenance, authentication and key agreement, formal protocol verification, security for AI and AI for security.

Research

I design and analyse cryptographic protocols. My current focus is making data provenance protocols such as TLS oracles hold up against quantum adversaries.

Cryptographic and decentralized protocols
Post-quantum cryptography, cryptographic protocol design and analysis, verifiable and distributed cryptographic protocols, data provenance protocols, and cryptographic privacy techniques.
Security and privacy in computer systems
Distributed systems security, blockchain and smart contracts, multi-party computation, and privacy in cloud computing.
AI for security, and security for AI
Secure and robust AI, privacy-preserving AI, privacy in machine learning, federated learning, and adversarial machine learning.
